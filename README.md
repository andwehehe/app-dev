# JoJo's Bizarre Adventure

> "Yare Yare Daze." — Jotaro Kujo

## About
JoJo's Bizarre Adventure is a Japanese manga and anime created by Hirohiko Araki. It follows the adventures of the Joestar family through different generations.

## Genres
- Action
- Adventure
- Supernatural
- Comedy
- Fantasy

| Part | Title | Main Character |

## What Makes It Special?
- Unique character designs and iconic poses.
- Creative and strategic Stand abilities.
- Memorable villains and epic battles.
- References to music, fashion, and pop culture.
- An unforgettable family legacy.
